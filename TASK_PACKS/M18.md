# M18 — Activity: Health Connect (activity only) + manual log
Depends on: GATE A   |   Refs: Master Spec §17 (no heart rate)

## In scope
- `HealthConnector` interface (§17.1) with three implementations:
  - `AndroidHealthConnectConnector` via `react-native-health-connect`, reading exercise sessions, steps, distance and active calories only;
  - `ManualActivityLog`;
  - `FakeConnector` for tests.
- Normalisation happens on the device into daily summaries, which are uploaded to `app.activity_daily_summaries`; sessions go to `app.activity_sessions`.
- Requires consent `HEALTH_CONNECT_ACTIVITY` + child assent. Disconnecting stops sync and deletes stored connector state.
- Age-group sport plan templates (non-medical) feed the M07 ActivityGoal and the M04 Tier 2 activity fact.
- The Android manifest declares only the 4 allowed permissions. The Play Console Health Connect declaration is drafted in `docs/legal/`.

## Out of scope
Heart rate, exercise routes/GPS, clinical data types.

## Acceptance
- With the fake connector and a real device, steps and one exercise session appear as a daily summary.
- Revoking consent stops sync.
- A manifest check passes in CI.
