# M17 — Language skills: writing feedback (Vietnamese + English), on-device speech
Depends on: GATE A, X03   |   Refs: Master Spec §15 (General + School English); docs/CONTENT_PLAN_vi.md; docs/COST_OPTIONS_vi.md §3

## Goal
Unlock `written` library items and add practice for the language libraries (Tiếng Việt 2, Ngữ văn 6, Tiếng Anh 2, Tiếng Anh 6). Reading items already work through M06 (passage groups).

## In scope
- Tables: `writing_submissions(item_id, text, rubric_scores, feedback, model, ai_run_id)`, `writing_skill_states`, `english_profiles`, `listening_attempts`, `speaking_attempts`, `english_skill_states`. English internal levels Pre-A1..B2, labelled "ước lượng luyện tập".
- **Writing feedback** for `written` items in all language libraries:
  - scored against the item's rubric, with highlights and suggestions, never a full rewrite;
  - Tiếng Việt 2: 3–5 sentences; gentle feedback on spelling, capital letters, punctuation and complete sentences;
  - Ngữ văn 6: a paragraph; feedback on topic sentence, ideas, connectors and spelling;
  - English: sentences or a short paragraph; grammar and vocabulary feedback;
  - model from `ai_task_config` (`claude-haiku-4-5` for G1-9, `claude-sonnet-5-5` for G10-12), realtime or nightly batch (parent setting);
  - requires AI consent and counts against the daily AI quota;
  - rubric scores feed `writing_skill_states`, not correct/incorrect mastery.
- **Grade 2 input**: the child types with a large keyboard, or a parent types what the child wrote on paper.
- **Listening** (English): on-device TTS via `expo-speech` (en-US/en-GB voices), with speed control. No cloud TTS.
- **Speaking** (English, plus Vietnamese read-aloud practice for Tiếng Việt 2): on-device speech recognition through a maintained Expo module (ADR picks the module). Recording starts only on tap and requires `MICROPHONE_SPEAKING` consent. The transcript is compared word by word with the target sentence. Audio never leaves the device.

## Out of scope
IELTS/TOEIC modes, phoneme-level scoring, cloud STT/TTS, handwriting recognition from photos.

## Acceptance
- A Tiếng Việt 2 written item and a Ngữ văn 6 paragraph get rubric feedback in Vietnamese; the cost appears in `ai_runs`.
- `written` items are hidden in M06 before this module and shown after, following the subject profile.
- One English session per skill updates English states independently of school mastery.
- Network inspection shows no audio upload.
