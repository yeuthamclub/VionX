# M19 — Arena (single-player)
Depends on: GATE A   |   Refs: Master Spec §18.1-18.3

## In scope
- `GamePlugin` interface with server-side validation.
- Tables: `app.game_sessions`, `app.game_skill_metrics`, `app.ratings`, and a `library.game_content` pool (puzzles are shared library content).
- Games:
  - Sudoku: generator with a unique-solution check and technique-based difficulty; generated puzzles are stored in the shared pool.
  - One logic family: nonogram or KenKen.
  - Chess puzzles from the Lichess open puzzle database (CC0), imported into the pool.
  - Vs-computer chess on the device (Stockfish via a WebView/WASM or native module, chosen in the ADR).
- Daily Arena time limit and XP caps through M04 rules.

## Out of scope
Multiplayer, online rating, coding sandbox, music, art.

## Acceptance
- A forged "solved" submission is rejected by the server.
- Arena XP stops at the cap.
- Puzzle rating updates after a session.
