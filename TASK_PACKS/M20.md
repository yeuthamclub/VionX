# M20 — Competitions
Depends on: GATE A   |   Refs: Master Spec §20

## In scope
- Competitions are global, shared data in the `library` schema; entities follow §20.1.
- Providers: `AdminManualProvider` and `CSVImportProvider`.
- `app.family_location_preferences` stores province/district only and requires consent `COMPETITION_AREA`.
- Matching:
  1. eligibility filter;
  2. score from reachability, subject fit, interest, readiness and deadline;
  3. template-based explanation (no AI).
- Participation and results tracking.
- Competitions not verified for 90 days are hidden.

## Acceptance
- With 20 seeded competitions, a Grade 6 child in Hà Nội with strong math sees eligible contests with reasons.
- The app never requests the location permission.
