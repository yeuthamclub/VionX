# M16 — Reading library
Depends on: GATE A   |   Refs: Master Spec §16 (no §16.2 eye tracking)

## In scope
- Tables: `app.books` (household-private imports) and `library.books` (public-domain/licensed shared books), plus `book_chapters`, `reading_goals`, `reading_sessions`, `reading_comprehension_attempts`.
- In-app EPUB reader (epub.js in WebView or a maintained RN EPUB reader, chosen in the PLAN) and PDF viewer.
- "Paper book" sessions: timer + page count.
- Comprehension questions per chapter are **library items** (shared, generated once via M14 for library books). For household-private books without questions, the parent does a quick check (yes/no + note).
- Reading quality indicator: completion 0.4 + comprehension 0.6, configurable and labelled as internal.
- Goals per §16.3.
- Events `ReadingSessionFinished` and `ComprehensionCompleted` feed M04 weekend tiers and M07.
- Household-imported books are private to the household and served only through signed URLs.

## Acceptance
A child reads 2 chapters of a public-domain EPUB, passes the comprehension check and meets the goal → the Tier 2 reading fact is true.
