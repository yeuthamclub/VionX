# M22 — Production readiness
Depends on: GATE A   |   Refs: ARCHITECTURE §6-7

## In scope
- **Parent phone OTP:** a Supabase Auth SMS provider that delivers to Vietnamese numbers (a supported provider, or the Send SMS hook to a Vietnamese brandname SMS vendor). Includes OTP rate limits and cost alerts.
- **Google Sign-In:** production OAuth client with the release SHA-1/SHA-256 fingerprints.
- **Environments:** separate Supabase staging and production projects, migration promotion runbook, secrets rotation runbook.
- **AI spend controls:** Anthropic workspace spend limit and per-task monthly budgets in `ai_task_config`. When a budget is hit, the worker pauses that task's batches and the tutor falls back to T0/T1.
- **Legal:** cross-border transfer impact assessment draft, final legal review of `docs/legal/`, and a residency ADR (stay in Singapore or move the database to a Vietnamese cloud).
- **Public pages** on the admin domain: privacy policy and account deletion.

## Acceptance
- The production §43 flow works with real Google login and a real OTP sent to a Vietnamese number.
- Hitting a budget cap triggers the documented fallback.
- Legal sign-off is recorded.
